# Prompt gambar (Codex image_generation)

Semua gambar adalah ILUSTRASI untuk web showcase Smart Tree (penghijauan Arafah). Tanpa teks, tanpa logo, tanpa orang.
Dibuat ulang dengan: `codex exec --enable image_generation -s workspace-write "<prompt di bawah>"` dari folder proyek.

## hero.png (bab intro, 16:9, 1920x1080 atau terdekat)
Wide landscape illustration of the plain of Arafah near Mecca at dusk: soft rocky granite hills in the far distance,
warm sand plain in the foreground, a gentle line of young green trees being planted across the sand suggesting
desert greening. Painterly, soft gradients, subtle grain, calm and hopeful mood. Palette: deep indigo-violet sky
(#12131f to #2a1f2e) glowing to warm gold (#c9a45c) at the horizon, cream highlights, olive and leaf greens
(#5f8f4e, #8fc46a). Large calm empty area in the center and upper half for a title overlay. No text, no people,
no buildings, no logos, no watermark.

## sky.png (latar scene 3D, 16:9)
Pure dusk sky gradient, no objects, no clouds or only extremely faint wisps: deep indigo-violet at the top
(#12131f) blending to plum (#2a1f2e) and a soft warm gold glow (#c9a45c) near the bottom edge. Smooth, painterly,
very subtle grain, no banding. No text, no land, no sun disc.

## sand.png (tekstur tanah 3D, 1:1, 1024x1024)
Top-down orthographic texture of fine warm desert sand, evenly lit, soft subtle ripples, no shadows from objects,
no footprints, no stones larger than grains, uniform color around #cdb892 slightly warmer. Tileable look,
no vignette.

## ornament.png (ornamen, 1:1, 1024x1024)
A single symmetric Islamic geometric rosette medallion (8-pointed khatam star with girih interlace), thin elegant
gold lines (#c9a45c) on a plain solid very dark background (#12131f), centered, flat vector-like line art,
generous margin around it. No text, no shading, no 3D.
